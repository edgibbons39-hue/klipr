Features

Everything happens on your PC.

No cloud rendering queue, no per-clip fees, no watching a progress bar. Klipr sits in your system tray and gets on with it.

Real-time chat spike detection

Klipr reads every message as it arrives and measures how hard chat reacts. When the rate jumps past your threshold, that's the moment — caught live, not scraped afterwards.

Vertical 9:16 with burned-in subtitles

Every clip is reframed to phone shape with a blurred background fill. Captions are transcribed locally with Whisper and burned into the video — nothing is sent to a third-party editor.

AI-generated headlines

Each clip gets a punchy title written from what was actually said, and Klipr learns from your real view counts to write better ones over time.

AI viral scoring PRO

Before anything goes out, every clip is rated 1–10 for viral potential — based on headline quality, how hard chat reacted, and replay value. Post the strong ones first.

YouTube Shorts. You connect your own channel during setup — all views, subscribers and revenue go directly to you. YouTube has a daily API upload limit of approximately 6 videos per day on a standard project quota.

YouTube shares ad revenue on Shorts with creators in the YouTube Partner Program — that is why Klipr targets it rather than TikTok, whose creator fund pays far less for the same views. You have to reach YouTube’s Partner Program thresholds first (1,000 subscribers plus 10 million public Shorts views in 90 days), and what you earn after that depends on your views and audience. Klipr’s job is to keep good clips going out every day so you get there faster. It cannot promise a figure, and anyone selling you one is guessing.
