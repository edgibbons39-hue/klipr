Features

Everything a clip channel needs. Nothing it doesn't.

A complete end-to-end pipeline built for one thing: turning streams into an endless feed of viral shorts.

Real-time chat spike detection

Klipr monitors live chat velocity on Kick and Twitch simultaneously. When the room explodes — spam, emotes, mass reactions — it knows a clip just happened and captures the moment before it's gone. Every streamer gets their own sensitivity threshold, so a chat that is always busy doesn't trigger constantly while a quiet one still registers.

Vertical 9:16 + burned-in subtitles

Every clip is automatically reframed to 9:16 with a blurred background fill. Accurate word-by-word captions are transcribed locally using Whisper and burned directly into the video — no third-party editor, no upload to external services.

AI-generated headlines

Every clip gets a punchy, click-worthy title generated from the actual transcript. Over time Klipr learns which headline styles perform best for each streamer and uses real view data to write better titles automatically.

AI viral scoring — before you upload PRO

Before anything goes live, Klipr's AI reviews every clip and rates it 1-10 for viral potential — based on headline quality, how hard chat reacted, streamer recognition, and replay value. Clips are ranked so you always know which ones to push first. The AI gets smarter over time by learning from your real view counts.

Smart upload scheduling PRO

Set the exact date and time each clip posts — separately for YouTube and TikTok — or let Klipr space them evenly across your posting window. Algorithms reward consistent posting, not bulk dumps.

Direct upload to YouTube Shorts

Connect your accounts once. Clips upload directly to YouTube Shorts under your own channel. TikTok direct posting is live for Pro — clips post straight to your TikTok account. Until TikTok finishes reviewing Klipr for public posting, clips arrive privately and you publish them with one tap. All views, subscribers, and revenue go straight to you.

Monitor multiple streamers at once

Watch 3 channels on Starter, or as many as you like on Pro. Each runs in its own independent loop across Kick and Twitch — no interference, no slowdown, no per-clip fees.

Runs silently, 24/7

A lightweight Windows desktop app that lives in your system tray. Minimal CPU usage. Start it once and it handles everything in the background — you'll forget it's running until clips start appearing.
